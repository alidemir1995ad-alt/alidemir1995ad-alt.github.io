I designed and taught Introductory Migration Economics, an English-language undergraduate seminar at Ruhr University Bochum, in winter semester 2025/26. I developed the syllabus and lecture materials, combining economic models of migration with the interpretation and evaluation of empirical research.

The seminar covered migrant selection (the Roy model), integration and human capital, Chiswick–Borjas earnings assimilation, empirical research designs, and labor-market effects.

As a Research Associate at the Chair of Empirical Economics (October 2023–September 2026), I also handled course administration for Econometric Evaluation of Economic Policies and co-supervised bachelor's and master's theses.

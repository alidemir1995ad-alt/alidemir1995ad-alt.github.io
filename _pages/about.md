I am an applied microeconomist working on **migration, housing and family formation**. I completed my PhD in Economics at **Ruhr University Bochum in October 2026**, supervised by Thomas K. Bauer and Arnaud Chevalier. My primary fields are labor and demographic economics, with secondary interests in urban and regional economics.

My job market paper, [**German Internal Migration: A Marriage Market Perspective**](/job-market-paper/), asks how East–West migration after German reunification affected marriage and divorce in receiving communities. I digitized records for 320 West German districts from 1985 to 2007 and compared districts within the same state using settlement ties recorded in the 1961 census. From the mid-1990s, divorce rates fell more in districts with stronger ties to the East. Comparing the districts in the top and bottom fifths of historical ties, the relative decline amounted to **14% of the mean divorce rate**. Marriage rates showed no corresponding increase.

My other [research](/research/) uses the German Socio-Economic Panel (SOEP) and local housing-market data (RWI-GEO-REDX) to study homeownership and the transition to a second birth, and local rents and young adults' departure from the parental home.

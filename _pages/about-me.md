I am an applied microeconomist. I completed my PhD in Economics at Ruhr University Bochum in October 2026, supervised by Thomas K. Bauer and Arnaud Chevalier. My dissertation is titled *Housing, Migration, and Family Formation*.

My primary research fields are labor and demographic economics, particularly migration, fertility and family formation. My secondary fields are urban and regional economics, with a focus on housing. My dissertation studies migration and housing markets in relation to divorce, fertility and leaving the parental home.

## Academic background

I hold a B.Sc. in Mathematics from Middle East Technical University and an M.Sc. in Behavioral and Applied Economics from the University of Trento, where I received a merit scholarship. During my master's studies, I spent an exchange year studying Public Economics at Freie Universität Berlin.

From July to October 2022, I was a research intern at the Vienna University of Economics and Business, hosted by Susann Fiedler at the Institute for Cognition and Behavior. I programmed an oTree bargaining experiment on overconfidence and motivated beliefs, which provided data for my master's thesis. I also worked as a student research assistant to Constantinos Hadjichristidis at the University of Trento from January to August 2022.

## Research and teaching experience

From October 2023 to September 2026, I was a Research Associate at Ruhr University Bochum's Chair of Empirical Economics and a member of DFG Research Training Group 2484, *Regional Disparities and Economic Policy*.

My doctoral work combines original historical data construction with longitudinal household analysis. I have experience digitizing regional statistical publications, harmonizing geographical boundaries, linking household and housing-market data, and working with restricted-access geocoded survey data. I use Stata, R and Python for data preparation and empirical analysis.

In winter semester 2025/26, I designed and taught *Introductory Migration Economics*, an English-language undergraduate seminar at Ruhr University Bochum, developing its syllabus and lecture materials. I also handled course administration for *Econometric Evaluation of Economic Policies* and co-supervised bachelor's and master's theses.

I am on the **2026–27 economics job market**, seeking postdoctoral and research economist positions in Germany.
